# Changing a workspace

You opened a file of a workspace. Most workspaces keep rules of their own in their `README.md`, under "Rules", with the gotchas learned there under "Gotchas"; `deploy/README.md` holds those of `deploy/` and of `.github/`. Read them before you change anything in that workspace. This file holds no rule of its own; it is here so that you read those.
